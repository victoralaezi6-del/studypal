Platform features

The essential tools for finding and organising academic resources

StudyPal brings searchable resources, department categories, saved materials, and student progress into one clean interface.

01 ⌕

Fast resource search

Search by course code, title, keyword, or department from the dashboard or complete resource library.

02 ▤

Department categories

Browse resources for Aircraft, Business, Civil, Electrical, and Mechanical Engineering.

03 ☆

Saved resources

Save important course materials so they remain easy to locate during future study sessions.

04 ◉

Activity dashboard

Monitor searches, viewed resources, saved items, study points, and daily study streaks.

05 ◐

Light and dark mode

Switch between themes across the landing page, account pages, and student dashboard.

06 ▣

Responsive dashboard

Use StudyPal comfortably on desktop computers, tablets, and mobile devices.
